FREELANCE DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Brightline Media LLP ("Company") and the independent designer named in the order form ("Contractor").

Clause 1 - Scope of Work. Contractor will design brand assets, social media creatives and a website mock-up as described in the statement of work attached to this Agreement.

Clause 2 - Acceptance. All deliverables are subject to approval by the Company at its absolute discretion. Company is not required to give reasons for rejecting any deliverable, and rejected work will not be paid for.

Clause 3 - Payment. Company will pay each undisputed invoice within one hundred twenty (120) days of receipt. No advance or milestone payments will be made.

Clause 4 - Delays. If any deliverable is delivered after the agreed date, Contractor shall pay liquidated damages of INR 10,000 for each day of delay, deducted from any amount owed.

Clause 5 - Intellectual Property. Contractor hereby assigns to Company all right, title and interest in every work created under this Agreement, including any templates, fonts, tools and know-how that Contractor developed before this Agreement.

Clause 6 - Indemnity. Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities and expenses arising out of or in connection with the services, howsoever caused.

Clause 7 - Termination. Company may terminate this Agreement at any time for any reason without prior notice. On termination Company will pay only for deliverables already accepted.

Clause 8 - Restrictions. For a period of five (5) years after this Agreement ends, Contractor shall not engage in any similar business or provide design services to any client of the Company anywhere in India.

Clause 9 - Confidentiality. Contractor shall keep all information received from Company strictly confidential in perpetuity. Company has no obligation to keep Contractor's information confidential.

Clause 10 - Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
